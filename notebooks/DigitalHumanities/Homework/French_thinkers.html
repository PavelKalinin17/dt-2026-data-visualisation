Finally, I decided to compare the eras and primary fields of activity of the philosophers in this dataset. This analysis reveals that the Enlightenment was characterized by philosophers working in political philosophy and materialism, whereas the modern period encompasses movements such as existentialism, phenomenology, and deconstruction. It can be concluded that during periods of persistent political turmoil, ideas related to that sphere flourished. Conversely, once that era had passed, thinkers turned their attention to more humanistic concepts a natural progression, given the need to reflect on past events.
